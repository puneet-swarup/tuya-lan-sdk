# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people. Reviewing a patch is not a licence
  to be dismissive.
- Assume the other person is acting in good faith, and say what you actually
  observed — a log, a trace, a failing test.
- Accept that "I do not know" and "I have not verified that" are complete
  answers. Guessing confidently about firmware behaviour wastes everyone's time.
- Respect that maintainers owe you a response, not a particular outcome.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, commits or
  private messages.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information, including credentials, without
  permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages — and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are handled privately.
Responses range from a private word to a temporary or permanent ban, depending on
severity and pattern.
